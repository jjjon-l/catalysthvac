Contact & Service Request Questions

How do I request residential HVAC service?
Use the residential service request form or call Catalyst. Include the service location and a short description of what's happening so the request can be routed correctly.
Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?
No. Submitting the form requests contact, and Catalyst confirms the next step with you.
What if I'm considering HVAC replacement?
Use the replacement request so Catalyst can capture the home and system details needed for the right consultation.
How do I contact Catalyst about commercial HVAC?
Use the Commercial HVAC Inquiry. Commercial requests are kept separate from residential service requests so they reach the right person.
How do I contact Catalyst about an apartment community or an industrial facility?
Use the Multi-Family HVAC Inquiry or the Industrial HVAC Inquiry. Each goes to the people who handle that work, separate from residential service requests.
How do I apply for a job at Catalyst?
Submit your resume on the Careers page. The service forms are for service requests only.
How do I know whether Catalyst serves my address?
Include your service address or ZIP in the request, or call Catalyst. The Service Areas page describes the broader footprint, but serviceability is confirmed for each address.
What are Catalyst's service hours?
Our office is open ____. Request service online any time and the team will follow up during business hours.
