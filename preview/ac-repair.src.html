AC Problems

When Your AC Isn't Working the Way It Should

You don't need to diagnose the system before requesting service. Start with what you're experiencing. Several different HVAC issues can create similar symptoms, so the purpose of the visit is to find out what's actually happening before deciding on the repair.

AC Running but Not Cooling

The system may be running without delivering the indoor temperature you expect. That symptom can have more than one cause, so it should be evaluated rather than diagnosed from the symptom alone.

Weak or Uneven Airflow

Low airflow, rooms that are hard to cool or noticeable differences across the home can involve the equipment, the airflow system, controls or other conditions that need to be evaluated together.

AC Turning On and Off Repeatedly

Frequent or unusual cycling can signal that the system is struggling to operate normally. The cause should be identified before assuming a particular component needs replacing.

Unusual Noise or System Behavior

New noises, vibration, unexpected shutdowns or other changes in how the system runs are good reasons to have it checked before the problem becomes more disruptive.

Thermostat or Control Issues

A thermostat setting, control problem or communication issue can sometimes look like an equipment failure. A proper evaluation treats the controls as part of the system.

Moisture, Drainage or Leaking Around the System

Water or moisture around HVAC equipment can have several causes and shouldn't be ignored. Catalyst can evaluate the source as part of the service call.

Repair or Replace?

A Repair Call Shouldn't Automatically Become a Replacement Call

If a system can be repaired reliably and the cost makes sense for its condition, repair may be the practical next step. Replacement becomes worth evaluating when the current problem is part of a larger pattern, or when the economics and condition of the system change the decision.

No single factor, especially age by itself, should be treated as an automatic answer. The decision should be based on the actual system and the options available.

AC Repair Questions

Why is my AC running but not cooling?
There are several possible causes, and the symptom alone isn't enough to identify the repair. The issue may involve the equipment, controls, airflow or another part of the cooling system. A service evaluation is the right way to find out what's actually happening.
Why is the airflow from my vents weak?
Weak airflow can have different causes, including conditions within the HVAC equipment or the home's airflow system. Because several problems can produce a similar symptom, the system should be evaluated before assuming a specific repair.
Why does my AC keep turning on and off?
Unusual or frequent cycling can happen for more than one reason. The important next step is identifying why the system is cycling that way and whether the issue involves controls, equipment operation, airflow or another condition.
Should I repair or replace my AC?
That depends on the current repair, overall system condition, recurring problems, performance, parts availability, and the cost and value of the available options. Age can be relevant, but it shouldn't be the only factor. If you have a current cooling problem and don't already know replacement is necessary, a repair evaluation is a reasonable place to start.
