Industrial Systems

Hydronic Systems, Fan Coils and VRV/VRF

Industrial and large commercial buildings often use systems that move heating and cooling differently from a typical rooftop unit. Catalyst services and installs them.

System

Hydronic Systems

Chilled-water and hot-water loops that move heating and cooling through a building by water, including pumps, valves, piping and controls.

System

Fan Coil Units

Terminal units that use the building's chilled or hot water to condition individual rooms and zones.

System

VRV/VRF Systems

Variable refrigerant systems that serve many zones from shared outdoor equipment, adjusting output zone by zone for efficient, precise control.

System

Packaged, Rooftop and Split Systems

The commercial equipment that serves offices, shops and support buildings on an industrial site.

Industrial HVAC Questions

What types of industrial facilities does Catalyst work on?
Energy offices and campuses, petrochemical and chemical facilities, electrical facilities and other industrial sites across Greater Houston.
Does Catalyst work on hydronic systems?
Yes. Catalyst services and installs hydronic systems, including chilled-water and hot-water loops and the fan coils they serve.
Does Catalyst install and service VRV/VRF systems?
Yes. Catalyst works on VRV/VRF systems for industrial and commercial buildings.
Can work be scheduled around shutdowns and outages?
Yes. Maintenance and replacement work is planned around the facility's outage windows and access requirements.
Does Catalyst offer industrial maintenance agreements?
Yes. Agreements are built around your equipment list, with scheduled visits and a written report after each one.
