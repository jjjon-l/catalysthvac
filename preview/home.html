Repair or Replace?

Not Every AC Problem Should Become a Replacement Conversation

The right answer depends on what the technician finds and what you want from the system going forward.

When repair makes sense

A repair may be the practical next step when the system can be restored reliably at a reasonable cost.

Need AC Repair? Start Here

When replacement is worth evaluating

When the current repair is part of a larger pattern, such as recurring failures, declining performance, difficult parts availability, comfort problems or repair costs that no longer make sense for the system.

HVAC Service Questions

Does Catalyst provide both residential and commercial HVAC service?
Yes. Catalyst's website separates residential service from commercial HVAC so homeowners and business or property decision-makers can move into the service path that fits their need.
What residential HVAC services does Catalyst offer?
Catalyst's primary residential services include AC repair, AC replacement and installation, heating repair and replacement, HVAC maintenance and indoor air quality.
What areas does Catalyst serve?
Catalyst serves the Greater Houston market, with a strong initial operating focus through South and Southeast Houston and the Bay Area. Visit the Service Areas page for current coverage and market-specific information.
Should I request AC repair or AC replacement?
If you are dealing with a current cooling problem and do not already know that replacement is necessary, AC repair is usually the clearer starting point. If the system has recurring problems, major repair needs or you are already evaluating a new system, the replacement page provides more decision support. A final recommendation should follow an evaluation of the actual system.
