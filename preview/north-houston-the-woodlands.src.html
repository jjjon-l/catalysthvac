Local Context

The Woodlands and a Growing Northern Corridor

The Woodlands has the strongest homeowner profile in the research: about 44,000 households, roughly 73% owner-occupied and a median home value above $500,000. Homeowners here weigh comfort, efficiency and long-term value carefully, and they expect clear options rather than a sales pitch.

Spring, Klein and Conroe add a mix of established neighborhoods and new construction along I-45 and the Grand Parkway, and the I-45 corridor carries a large share of North Houston's commercial and multi-family property.
