Why Insulation Matters

What the Right Attic Insulation Does for Your Home

Lower Energy Use

A properly insulated attic slows heat transfer, keeping the home cooler in summer and warmer in winter, which reduces strain on the HVAC system and can lower energy bills.

More Even Comfort

Less heat moving through the ceiling means fewer hot spots upstairs in summer and fewer chilly drafts in winter.

Less Strain on the HVAC System

When less heat reaches the living space, the air conditioner doesn't have to run as hard or as long to keep up.

Fewer Drafts From the Attic

Combined with air sealing, insulation helps limit outside air, dust and moisture coming in through the attic.

A Quieter Home

Insulation absorbs sound, reducing noise between floors and rooms.

Added Home Value

Energy-efficient upgrades appeal to buyers looking for long-term savings and comfort.

Insulation Options

Choosing the Right Type of Insulation

Insulation is rated by R-value, a measure of how well it resists heat flow. R-value depends on the material, its thickness and its density. A higher R-value means better resistance, and usually a higher cost. The right choice depends on the attic, the insulation already there and your budget.

Option

Fiberglass

Made from spun glass fibers and available as batts, rolls or loose fill. Affordable, durable and fire resistant, and suited to many attic layouts.

Option

Cellulose

Made from recycled paper treated with fire retardants and blown into the attic. Strong thermal performance and sound absorption, and an eco-friendly option.

Option

Spray Foam

Expands to fill gaps, cracks and irregular spaces, sealing air leaks while it insulates. Strong thermal resistance and moisture control, typically at a higher cost.

Attic Insulation Questions

How do I know if my attic needs more insulation?
Common signs include upstairs rooms that stay warm, an air conditioner that runs constantly in summer, uneven temperatures between rooms and energy bills that keep climbing. An attic assessment shows how much insulation is there now and whether air leaks or ventilation also need attention.
What is R-value?
R-value measures how well insulation resists heat flow. It depends on the material, the thickness and the density; a higher R-value means better resistance.
Which type of insulation is best for my attic?
It depends on the attic, the existing insulation and your budget. Fiberglass and cellulose are cost-effective for most attics, while spray foam also seals gaps and irregular spaces, usually at a higher cost.
Can attic insulation lower my energy bills?
Insulation reduces how much attic heat reaches the living space, which lowers the load on your cooling system. How much it helps depends on the home, the current insulation and the HVAC system.
