Local Context

From the Ship Channel to the Coast

This region covers established neighborhoods close to the city, fast-growing homeowner communities like Pearland and League City, and coastal towns where humid, salty air is hard on outdoor equipment. Long cooling seasons make maintenance and well-planned replacements especially valuable here.

It also includes the Port Houston and Ship Channel corridor through Pasadena, Deer Park, La Porte and Texas City, one of the largest concentrations of industrial and commercial facilities in the region, where Catalyst's commercial and industrial work fits naturally.
