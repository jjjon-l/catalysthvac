Around Catalyst's home base, Pasadena, Pearland, Friendswood, Clear Lake/Webster, League City and South/Southeast Houston also have their own pages.

Does Catalyst serve Dickinson?
Yes. Dickinson is served along with League City. Request service with your address to confirm.
Does Catalyst serve communities without their own page?
Often, yes. A website page isn't the same thing as a service boundary. If your community isn't listed, request service or call Catalyst to confirm coverage for your address.
Does Catalyst have an office in every city on this page?
No. These pages describe markets Catalyst serves, not separate Catalyst locations.
What residential HVAC services are available across the service area?
AC repair, AC replacement and installation, heating repair, heating replacement and installation, HVAC maintenance and attic insulation, along with multi-family, commercial and industrial HVAC.

Yes. Catalyst serves all of Greater Houston, and each of these communities is covered on its regional page. Request service with your address and the team will confirm the details.
