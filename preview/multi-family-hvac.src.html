Multi-Family HVAC

Multi-Family HVAC for Greater Houston Apartment Communities

Multi-family is where Catalyst A/C & Heat started. Apartment communities have their own HVAC realities: dozens or hundreds of systems on one property, residents who need cooling back quickly, unit turns on a deadline and budgets that have to hold across a whole portfolio.

Catalyst works with property management companies and owners to keep those systems running, respond when something fails and plan replacements before they turn into emergencies.

Built for Apartment Communities

Apartment HVAC Is Its Own Kind of Work

A system that works fine in a single-family home can be a challenge in an apartment building. Catalyst plans multi-family work around how each property was built and how it operates.

Unique Building Layouts

Equipment can sit on roofs, in closets, attics and mechanical rooms, with line sets and drains routed through occupied buildings. Every property gets a plan that fits its layout.

Occupied Units

Work is scheduled around residents, with notice and access coordinated through the property team.

Many Systems, Similar Ages

When most of a property's equipment was installed at the same time, repair-versus-replace decisions are made at the property level, not one failed unit at a time.

Permits and Inspections

Catalyst works with the city on permits, so replacement work passes inspection and stays on schedule.

One Accountable HVAC Partner

A Better Model Than the Old Supply-House Approach

For years, many properties relied on a patchwork: an on-site maintenance tech buying parts at the supply house, a different contractor for each emergency and equipment decisions made one failure at a time. That approach costs time, creates inconsistent installations and makes it hard to plan a budget.

Catalyst gives a property, or a whole portfolio, one HVAC partner that knows the equipment, keeps the records and plans ahead.

Multi-Family HVAC Questions

Does Catalyst work with property management companies?
