Consider the Full System

Comfort Problems Don't Always Have a Single Obvious Cause

If cooling varies between rooms, the system runs differently than before, or airflow feels inconsistent, describe the symptoms before deciding on a solution. Home comfort depends on more than one piece of equipment and can involve controls, airflow, distribution and other factors specific to your home.

That doesn't mean every comfort issue needs new equipment. A good service conversation separates what you've noticed from what a technician has actually confirmed.

Keep Repair and Replacement Decisions Separate

If your AC has stopped working as expected, start with AC Repair. If the concern is recurring trouble, equipment condition, future investment or whole-home comfort, ask about an AC Replacement & Installation evaluation. Options should be based on real findings, not blanket claims about how old a unit "should" be.
